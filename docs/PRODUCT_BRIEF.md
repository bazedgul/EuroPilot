# Product Brief

## Status

Proposed; unvalidated product hypothesis.

## Problem

Payment-related records can be delayed, duplicated, contradictory, or incomplete. Operators need a traceable way to correlate records, see an evidence timeline, classify incidents deterministically, and understand what remains unknown.

## Initial product

EuroPilot is an investigation and reconciliation aid using synthetic data and simulated provider events. It presents correlated attempts, events, invoices, evidence, findings, and uncertainty. AI may explain supplied evidence but is not authoritative.

## Boundaries

The MVP is read-only and must not execute payments, move money, approve transactions, connect production accounts, or claim to cancel/reverse/refund a submitted payment. Production readiness, market demand, and revenue potential are unvalidated.

## Users and success signals

Proposed users are operators investigating payment incidents. Phase 0 success is documentation consistency and owner approval, not customer or operational validation. User research and measurable product metrics are deferred.
